# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Thai and English-speaking staff, Accounting and department managers reviewing company budgets and supporting purchase documents.

## Product Purpose
BMS manages internal approval documents and presents traceable ERP budgets. The dashboard answers how much each department has, has consumed, has reserved and can still use, then opens the underlying evidence.

## Brand Personality
Clear, restrained, dependable. Keep the existing BMS shell, typography and controls. The owner requests a minimal, readable interface with complete and accurate data.

## Anti-references
Avoid dense ERP screens, repeated summary panels, nested dialogs, decorative charts and displays that silently equate purchase orders with actual budget consumption.

## Design Principles
- Summaries must reconcile with their details or explicitly identify the difference.
- Retain company, department and period context while navigating.
- Use distinct labels for budgets, purchases and consumption.
- Preserve unclassified data and communicate its scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Thai and English interface text, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, numeric labels alongside colors and reduced-motion support.
